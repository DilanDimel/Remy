# How RemyAI sounds

## Who is talking

Founders of RemyAI, a real company, talking to college students like a friend one or two years ahead who figured something out. Never a "student project", never a corporate brand voice.

## The voice

- Plain words, short sentences, one idea per line. Say it the way you'd text it.
- Start with the student's real problem or the result, never with "Introducing" or the logo.
- Specific beats clever: "60 applications, 2 replies" beats "the job hunt is tough".
- Show Remy doing one job (fixing one resume line, finding one opening you qualify for), not a feature list.
- Honest about what Remy is: a coach that never makes things up. Rewrites only use facts already on the resume, and the student accepts, edits or skips every change.
- One call to action at the end. Usually "Free during beta" and remyai.org.
- A little personality is good (the fox, a dry joke). Hype is not.

## Hard limits (the checker enforces most of these)

- Pricing: only "Free during beta". Never "free coach", "always free", "100% free" or "free forever".
- No CREATE program mention before Dec 1 2026, 12 PM CT.
- Don't describe RemyAI as a student project or a TCU project.
- No invented numbers, users, results or quotes. Every number needs a source we can show.
- Don't say Remy sends anything for the student. Outreach opens in their own mail app.
- Don't mention locked or owners-only features (Interview, Community, the phone app) as available.
- Don't say we track students or use analytics trackers (the Privacy page promises none).
- No em dashes or en dashes, no "it's not X, it's Y", no "no X, no Y, no Z" lists, no "game-changer / unlock / elevate / seamless / supercharge / delve", no rhetorical "Here's the thing", no emoji walls or hashtag walls (3 hashtags max, 2 emoji max).
