Honesty notes for judges: the "47 tabs / 12 boards" hook is an illustration, not a measured statistic. The inbox alerts in scene 2 are made-up postings modeled on the kind of irrelevant alerts students get; no real recruiters or companies are shown. Resume bullets, cover-letter text, and ATS numbers are sample values. Roadmap items are labeled "Coming next" and are not built.
