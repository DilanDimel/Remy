Resume accuracy push (Dilan, Nov 25, thread "Make Remy accurate on any resume"). A 1,000-resume test set (450 synthetic student resumes, 350 fictional professional resumes from the CC BY 4.0 dataset sukhrobnurali/resume-parsing-vision, 200 anonymized real-world texts from the Apache-2.0 dataset sandeeppanem/resume-json-extraction-5k) rendered as PDF, Word and pasted text, scored field by field against ground truth. Not committed (size and dataset terms); the harness and findings are in the proposal.

PR 1 (profile facts, everyone): `majorsOf` reads every common degree form (MS in, BSEd, BFA, BSN → Nursing, Diploma in, Associate of Applied Science in, Double Major in); `primaryEducation` accepts schools named without University/College when the entry names a degree (Virginia Tech, institutes, academies, Hochschule); `schoolName` keeps the campus ("University of California, Los Angeles"); `gradDate` reads "12/2026". With a perfect AI reading, profile facts went from 71% to 98% fully right on the set (school 82→100%, major 74→99%, graduation 92→99%).
Coordinated with the "Expand job coverage to the whole US" thread: it owns job sourcing and will build `shared/soc.ts` (title → O*NET-SOC); this thread owns `server/resume/*`, `server/jobs/fit.ts`, `ranking.ts`, `shared/roleMatch.ts`.

PR 1 merged as #453. PR 2 (PDF reading, everyone): `parse.ts` `markIndentedBullets` turns lines indented from a shared left edge into bullets when a PDF page prints no bullet characters (browser, Canva, some templates draw the dot as a shape), joining wrapped lines; pages with bullet characters are untouched. `rulesParse` finds an email, phone or link line printed below the first heading (sidebars read after the main column) and pulls contact details apart when run together with spaces or " — ". On the set (built-in reader): email 92→100%, student phone 64→100%, PDF bullets under the right entry 31→51%. The AI reader gets the same marked text.

PR 2 merged as #454. PR 3 (job matching, everyone): `fit.ts` `FIELDS` title lists widened to the openings each major really takes (accounting: bookkeeping; business: marketing, finance, HR, supply chain, sales, consulting, brand; communications: PR, marketing, social; public safety: legal, paralegal, court, clerk; data and IS: developer, ML, web, help desk; and a few more). `FIT_RULES_VERSION` 21. On 387 student resumes against a 74-job pool with hand-labelled relevant titles: a relevant opening in the top 5 went 82→91%, the student's own-field openings hidden as "not your field" went 16.6→1.4%, other-field openings shown 9.8→9.5% (no added noise), majors with no field recognized 7.2→2.8% (Spanish, History, Philosophy remain).

Real-resume study (Dilan, Nov 25, 3:55 AM CT: "go find 1,000 real resumes online, students and people 5-10+ years out"). Downloaded (local only, never committed): 2,484 US resumes (opensporks/resumes, CC0, the LiveCareer set: text-rendered PDFs in 24 job categories), 1,940 real designed PDFs (d4rk3r/resumes-raw-pdf, MIT; mostly Vietnamese, 46% English, many with photos and partly redacted), 104 sample pages from 16 US university career-center resume guides, and the Djinni candidate-profile set (lang-uk, MIT, with years of experience). Career-center student samples: 100% one page, median 349 words, summary 23%, photo 0%, GPA 66%. US people past school: 1-2 pages for 73-88%, summary 75-85%. Designed PDFs: 70% photo, 48% personal details (birthday, gender), 56% three or more pages; 18% have no text layer at all (Remy rejects those as scans). Remy's reader on them: 93% (career samples), 97% (US), 82% (designed) readable; today's suggestions never mention a photo or personal details and say nothing on half of 3+ page designed resumes.

PR 4 (format check, brand-new, owners first): `server/resume/format.ts` (`pdfLayout`: pages, headshot-like picture, side-by-side columns, body text size, margin; `formatCheck`: pages for the level, photo, personal details as labels, columns, tiny text, crowded edges, word count, summary advice, "references available"). Routes `GET /api/resume/format`, `GET /api/resume/format/preview?look=`, `POST /api/resume/format` (owners only). `ResumeStyle.look` (classic, modern, compact) makes downloads use Remy's clean one-column layout in that format (`docs.ts` `renderResume`); "Keep my own file" clears it. Website: `client/src/components/FormatCheck.tsx` card beside the resume and a picker in place of it (owner view, ResumeOnePage). App: `native/src/components/FormatCard.tsx` on the Resume tab (owners only), formats as a page sheet with Preview (phone's PDF viewer) and Use it. Noted for Demetre: the native change is one new component and one line in `(tabs)/resume.tsx`. On the real set: flags something on 96% of designed photo or multi-page resumes, stays quiet on 75% of career-center samples (the rest get a tip or a real issue).

PR 5 (scanned resumes, Dilan's go-ahead Nov 25, 4:37 AM CT; straight to students as an improvement to uploads): a PDF with under 200 letters of text (a scan or photo; 18% of the real designed set) is now sent to the AI as the PDF itself (`scanText` in `server/resume/parse.ts`, OpenRouter file part), which copies the words exactly; the result goes through the usual reading and the student's "Does this look like your resume?" check, with a warning that it came from a scan. Uses the existing AI rate limits and 5 MB upload cap. No AI available or nothing readable: the old "can't read a scan" message. `aiText` now accepts a content array as the user message.
