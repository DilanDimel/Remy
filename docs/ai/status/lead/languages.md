Remy in more languages (Dilan, Nov 27; thread "Make Remy work in more languages"; proposal https://claude.ai/artifact/4MQ2pibNmDg7RRjj3V944V, Dilan said go). Phase 0, a fix for everyone: `server/resume/langs.ts` has section headings, months, "present", degree words and majors in the 11 most spoken US languages after English. Used by `headingOf`/`kindOf`, `tokens` (Unicode, Chinese read two characters at a time), `graduationOf`, `degreeField`/`primaryEducation` in `server/resume/resume.ts`, `norm` in `server/resume/parse.ts` (keeps every letter), `chosenFieldText` in `server/jobs/fit.ts` (adds the English major), the ATS rename tip in `server/resume/ats.ts`, and `otherWords` in `server/ai/claims.ts` (invented words in Chinese, Arabic, Korean, Hindi, Russian, Vietnamese are flagged). Test: 10,008 synthetic resumes, 12 languages, `/mnt/project-files/languages/test-harness/`.
