Voice (Dilan, Nov 24): the `Permissions-Policy` header (`server/index.ts`) said `microphone=()`, so every browser refused the mic and "Answer out loud", "Practice out loud" and the Remy chat/office mic failed silently (SpeechRecognition error `not-allowed`, swallowed by `onerror = () => {}`). Now `microphone=(self)`. Mic errors show plain words (`micProblem` in `client/src/lib/voice.ts`); "Hear it" in Interview becomes Stop while speaking and says so if the browser can't speak. Note: the Claude app's built-in browser pane blocks the mic on its own, so test the mic in real Chrome or Edge. Firefox has no speech recognition (buttons hide or say "type instead").
