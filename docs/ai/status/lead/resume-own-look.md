Resume keeps the student's own look (Dilan, Nov 24, everyone): PDF uploads are redrawn as their own PDF after edits (`server/resume/pdfTwin.ts`, bundled OFL metric fonts in `server/fonts/`, `fontkit`); only changed lines move, added lines fall back to Remy's drawing with a note. Word keeps its own file even with unsaved edits (`POST /api/resume/current-file`, replaces `GET /api/resume/current-docx`). PDF and Word downloads use the student's own layout when the format matches the upload; a PDF upload's Word download is a real-paragraph Word copy in the PDF's look (`server/resume/twinDocx.ts`), bullets added in Remy go into the PDF under the bullet before them, and pictures (logos, photos) are carried into both. A Word upload's PDF download is laid out in the browser from their own Word file (`client/src/lib/wordPdf.ts`, same drawing as the page preview) and drawn by `POST /api/resume/word-pdf` (`twinFromLayout`); `download(url, name, body)` now POSTs a body (contract check knows). Name, contact, heading, role, date and skills edits are placed in the PDF too: `fileFields` on the base resume (by `fieldsOf` key, set at upload, backfilled on the next save) says what each field read in the file; a field that shows more than once is never guessed. A role's first bullet goes under the role's lowest line shaped like another file bullet; overflow moves onto the next page (`flow`, a role stays with its text), and `clashes` refuses any rebuilt page with overlapping or off-page text. One-page PDF resumes stay one page (Dilan, Nov 24): `fit` tightens spacing up to 10%; past that the page reports `X-Remy-Over` lines and the Resume page offers the weakest bullets to cut. "No number" now shows once per role (`issues(text, doc)` in `shared/markup.ts`), Fix with Remy asks for numbers instead of guessing and says why a rewrite is better.
