Sign-up and setup redesign from Dilan's Nov 27 walkthrough (mockups + proposal approved Nov 27, 9:55 PM CT; everyone, both views). Six PRs in order: 1) account and saving fixes, 2) transcript reader, 3) Opportunities location and scrolling, 4) sign-in and setup restyle, 5) My Path coaching (owners first), 6) recorded narration (owners first).

PR 1: `DELETE /api/ws` removes every google/supabase account with the same sign-in email (`accountsWithEmail` in `server/platform/db.ts`); `PUT /api/ws/onboarding` keeps saved answers when a stale step change sends fewer for the same questions; setup Continue waits for the last answer's save; quick-check counter counts from the live list; `fullerName` (`shared/names.ts`) takes the resume's name when it only adds middle names; facts from the student's own imported documents are approved unless they conflict. App (`native/`, read demetre.md first, no claims on these files): the same Continue and counter changes in `native/src/components/setup/Questions.tsx` and `Review.tsx`.

PR 2: transcripts get their own reader (`server/resume/transcript.ts`: `TRANSCRIPT_READER` instructions, 40,000-character window, up to 25 facts, and `numbersPrinted` drops any GPA or credit figure not printed on the transcript). `ProfileImport` is PDF only for transcripts, retries once and shows a clear error; the setup side panel says which file it's reading. The app has no transcript upload (website only), but it uses the same server reader.

PR 3: "Fort Worth" and "Dallas" now mean the city (`METRO` in `server/jobs/fit.ts`); "DFW area", or picking both, means the metro. `relocateReason` never offers a job abroad. `FIT_RULES_VERSION` 26. Opportunities' "Only …" toggle hides every opening whose `regionMatch` is false (relocate ones included). `regionHint` (`shared/regions.ts`) explains the choice under the place chips in setup, website and app (`native/src/components/setup/Preferences.tsx`, noted per demetre.md). The tour's shade passes the mouse wheel to the page under it (`scrollUnder` in `Tour.tsx`), and the Opportunities list panel fades at its edges.

PR 4: sign-in (`.auth-stage`) and setup (`.ob`, `.ob-owner`) move from the black stage to the front page's paper, grid and orange glow (one CSS block at the end of `client/src/index.css`, tokens only), with a warm band at the top of each step card, orange chips, an orange Continue and an orange step list. Setup: Back on the resume step (reopens the goal, `regoal` in `Onboarding.tsx`); Edit on "Check my notes" opens the box in place with Save and Cancel (`ProfileForm` compact mode); LinkedIn's button says "Upload PDF" with how to get the PDF; `Q` has no number and a `why` line; the year shows as "You're a sophomore… Change" when already known; `setupReply` (`shared/setupReply.ts`) answers like a coach. Owners also get a "Say it" mic on the Questions step (`useVoice`). Welcome's email placeholder is "Your email" (demetre-5). App: question numbers removed in `native/src/components/setup/common.tsx` (noted per demetre.md); the app already had Back on every step.

PR 5: My Path coaching. Everyone: the walk-through (`useGuide` in `PathBoard.tsx`) no longer says "where you're headed" when the student is exploring (`onboarding.exploring` or more than two fields picked); each skill is explained in plain words (`shared/plainSkills.ts`, also at the top of a skill's details in `StopDetail`); business fields whose road lists no skills get Excel, financial models and PowerPoint (`buildStops`); certifications a student can earn now (`STARTER_CERTS`/`starterCertsFor` in `shared/roads.ts`: Excel MOS, Google Project Management, Google Data Analytics, Bloomberg Market Concepts for finance-like fields) sit in the student's own year, and licences that come after hiring sit under a "Later, after college" divider; the walk-through names only a certification worth doing now. The tour lands on My Path when finished or ended (`end` in `Tour.tsx`), and "Skip the tour" in setup goes to `/path`. Owners only (new): explore lanes on the Today panel (`ExploreLanes`, `shared/exploreLanes.ts`). The app has no My Path walk-through, certificate list or tour, so nothing to change there.

PR 6: recorded narration (owners only, new). `useNarration` in `client/src/lib/voice.ts` speaks a fixed line through the owners-only `/api/voice` (each line is cached in R2 by its text, so replays cost nothing); mute is per browser (`remy:narration` in localStorage). The tour bubble reads each step (`button-tour-voice`) and the owner setup notebook reads each chapter (`button-setup-voice`); on-screen text is the caption. The app has no tour, so nothing to change there.
