PR 11, Jobs swipe back on Android (Dilan, Nov 25 5:45 UTC: "find why android is not working and fix it"): root cause was PR 9's `dragOffsetFromRight={30}`. ReanimatedSwipeable passes `activeOffsetX: [dragOffsetFromRight, dragOffsetFromLeft]`, and the right offset must be negative; [30, 30] activated the pan on the first touch movement, so every vertical drag became a card swipe.
